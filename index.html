About Smoke Boys

Born in Pietermaritzburg, Inspired by Braai Culture

Smoke Boys started as a weekend fire in Imbali Unit 1, where family recipes met township hustle. Today we still cook the KZN way – patient smoke, bold spice and a generous hand with the basting.

Our ribs and chickens soak in marinades spiked with Durban masala, bushveld honey and citrus. They slow dance over wattle wood until the meat slips from the bone, then land on your plate with hearty sides your gogo would approve of.

  • 12-hour marinated ribs finished over open flames
  • Backyard-style smoked chicken with peri-peri swagger
  • Proudly South African sides, sauces and hospitality
